About

I’m an illustrator based in Japan, creating character-centered artwork with a focus on visual impact and atmosphere.

My work highlights expressive poses, thoughtful composition, and detailed costume design—often featuring VTubers, anime, and game characters from Japan and abroad.

For commissions, I welcome requests from VTubers and individuals with original avatars, including those active in VRChat or gaming communities. I’m also open to inquiries from companies.

Fanart of existing anime or game characters is created solely for non-commercial purposes.
