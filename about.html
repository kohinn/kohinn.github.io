ABOUT

Profile

Illustrator / Character Art

Kohinn

I am a character illustrator who loves anime, games, and VTubers, creating both fan art and original character illustrations.

My goal is to draw characters in a way that makes viewers think, "Yes, they would totally make that face!" I love capturing each character's unique personality and vibe through expressive faces, gestures, and poses.

Commission Process

  1. Inquiry & Quote

    Please reach out via the contact form. I will provide a price quote and estimated turnaround time based on your request.

  2. Rough Draft Check

    You will review the rough sketch to confirm the overall direction. Please let me know if you need any major changes at this stage.

  3. Final Rendering & Review

    After completing the artwork, you will do a final check. Minor adjustments can be made at this point.

  4. Delivery

    Once approved, I will deliver the final high-resolution files.

I usually reply to inquiries within 3 days. Please check the Contact page for pricing details.
