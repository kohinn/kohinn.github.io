Artist Statement

I value expressions that naturally convey a character’s charm and emotions.
Through facial expressions, gestures, lighting, and color, I aim to create illustrations that capture a moment of atmosphere and story.

I cherish the unique worldview that can be expressed through a single illustration,
and strive to create artwork that remains in the viewer’s memory.
