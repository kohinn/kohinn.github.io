ABOUT

Profile & Workflow

Illustrator / Character Art

Kohinn

I draw character illustrations. I love anime, games, and VTubers, and I create both fan art and original character artwork.

I aim to create illustrations that make people think, "That's totally the expression this character would make!" I enjoy capturing each character's personality and vibe through their expressions, gestures, and poses.

Commission Process

  1. Inquiry & Quote

    Please reach out via the contact form or DM. I will provide a quote and estimated turnaround based on your request.

  2. Rough Sketch Check

    You will review the rough sketch to confirm the overall direction. Please let me know if you need any major changes at this stage.

  3. Line Art, Coloring & Final Check

    After finishing the artwork, you will do a final review. Minor adjustments can be made here.

  4. Delivery

    I will deliver the completed high-resolution files.

I usually reply to inquiries within 3 days. Please check Contact for full pricing details.
