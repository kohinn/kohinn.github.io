ABOUT

Profile

Kohinn
Illustrator / Character Animator

I create anime-inspired character illustrations, mainly focusing on single-piece artworks.
I value expressions that naturally convey a character’s charm, emotions, and worldview.

Artist Statement

I believe that a single illustration has the power to convey a character's charm, story, and the atmosphere of a particular moment.

Through expressions, gestures, poses, lighting, and color, I focus on creating artwork that naturally communicates each character's personality and emotions.

I create character-focused illustrations across various fields, including VTuber, games, anime, and merchandise, with the goal of delivering artwork that leaves a lasting impression on viewers.
