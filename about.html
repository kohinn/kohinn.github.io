Artist Statement

I value expressions that naturally convey a character’s charm and emotions. Through facial expressions, gestures, lighting, and color, I aim to capture a moment of atmosphere and story within a single illustration.

I strive to create artwork that leaves a lasting impression and communicates a unique world through a single image.
