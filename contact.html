Usage Scope

  • Personal streaming & social media use (including monetized streams): Covered within the base price

R-18 / NSFW Commissions

  • Available as an add-on option (see pricing above)
  • Clients must be 18 years of age or older
  • Characters depicted must be adults
  • Samples are not publicly displayed; they can be shared upon inquiry

Content I Cannot Accept

  • Excessive violence or gore
  • R-18 / NSFW depictions of characters who appear underage
  • Fan art or derivatives based on specific real-life individuals
  • Any other content offensive to public order and morals

Mildly suggestive or all-ages themes—such as swimsuits, stylish outfits, or poses emphasizing body lines—are open for discussion.

Fan art commissions of existing works or characters are accepted only within the scope permitted by the respective rights holders' guidelines.
